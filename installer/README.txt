Inventory Manager
=================

Open the app from the "Inventory Manager" shortcut on the desktop or in the Start menu.

Your data (products, sales, customers, settings) is saved in:
    %AppData%\InventoryManager\inventory.db
Use Settings > Back Up Now to keep a copy on a USB drive or cloud folder.

To remove the app: Control Panel > Programs and Features > Inventory Manager > Uninstall.
Uninstalling does not delete your data.
